Privacy Policy

OT Knowledge Chomp · Last updated 20 November 2026

OT Knowledge Chomp ("the app") is published by MDAppz. This policy explains what the app collects, what it does not, and who else receives data when you play.

The short version

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. It never asks for your name, email address, or any other personal detail. Everything you do in the game — your progress, stars, streaks, settings and high-score initials — is stored only on your own device. The app itself operates no servers and transmits none of that anywhere.

The app does show advertising and does offer one optional purchase. Ads are handled by Google; the purchase is handled by Google Play on Android or by Apple's App Store on iPhone. That is where data leaves your device.

This information is held in the app's private storage. It is not uploaded, not shared, and not visible to us. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes all of it permanently. There is also a "Reset progress" option inside the pause menu.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To select and measure ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, general device and app information, coarse location inferred from your IP address, and interactions with the ads shown.

We do not receive, store, or have access to any of this data — it goes to Google, not to us. Google's handling of it is described in the Google Privacy & Terms and the AdMob documentation.

On iPhone, the app asks your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before your advertising identifier can be used. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", it is not shared and ads are not personalised.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent message before personalised ads are served. You can change your choice at any time from How to Play → Privacy Choices in the app.

You can also limit ad personalisation in your device settings: under Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android devices, or Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on iPhone. Buying the ad-free upgrade removes all advertising from the app.

Purchases (Google Play and the App Store)

The app offers a single optional purchase that removes advertising and unlocks a bonus level. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play on Android or by Apple's App Store on iPhone. We never see or handle your payment details. The app stores only a yes/no flag on your device recording that the purchase was made.

Crash diagnostics

The app includes Firebase Crashlytics so that crashes can be diagnosed and fixed. Where it is active, it may collect anonymous crash reports containing device model, operating system version, and the technical state of the app at the moment it failed. These reports contain no personal information and are not used to identify you.

Children

The app is a general-audience educational game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will address it.

Your choices

  • Reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings.
  • On iPhone, allow or deny tracking for the app under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • In the EEA, UK or Switzerland, change your ad consent from How to Play → Privacy Choices.
  • Remove advertising entirely by buying the ad-free upgrade.
  • Delete everything the app has stored by clearing its data or uninstalling it.
